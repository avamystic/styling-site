# Find Your Season | Color Analysis Quiz

A seasonal color analysis quiz that determines your best color palette — Spring, Summer, Autumn, or Winter — based on your natural coloring. Styled after Vogue's editorial aesthetic.

## How It Works

Answer 7 questions about your skin tone, hair color, eye color, and color preferences. The quiz scores your answers against seasonal profiles and reveals your personalized palette with:

- Your season's signature color palette
- Styling advice and colors to avoid
- Celebrity matches who share your coloring
- A saveable palette card

## Tech Stack

Pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript in a single file — no frameworks, no build tools, no dependencies beyond Google Fonts (CDN).

## Running Locally

Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve it:

```
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

## Design

- **Typography**: Playfair Display (headings), Cormorant Garamond (body), Inter (UI)
- **Layout**: Responsive, with a breakpoint at 600px
- **Animations**: Smooth fade-up transitions with editorial photo breaks
