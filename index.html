About

Style is the translation.
Identity is the original.

Twenty years in style taught me one thing: the clothes are never really the point.

I'm a lifelong visual artist—BFA in Fashion Design, years spent in New York's tech and startup world—and I've spent my career watching how people use what they wear to say something about who they are. Or who they're trying to become.

My process starts with identity, not inventory. Before we open a single drawer or walk into a single store, we talk. About you. About the gap between how you're seen and how you see yourself. About what you want your presence to say.

I work with established professionals—often in their 30s and 40s—whose outside no longer matches their inside. People in transition: a new role, a new chapter, a new sense of self. Style, for me, is a tool for that transition, not a costume for performance.

I don't tell you what to wear. Through my process, you tell me.
