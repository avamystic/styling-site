About

Style is more than the clothes you wear.

I have spent more than twenty years in style, working with people who are stepping into something new. A new role. A new chapter. A sense of self that has shifted and a wardrobe that has not caught up.

My practice is built around a signature process designed to surface your own point of view, not impose mine. We work from the inside out: who you are, how you want to be seen, and what it takes to close that gap.

I am a lifelong visual artist with a BFA in Fashion Design, and I have spent years working across New York's tech and startup world. That background matters. I understand the pace, the range of contexts you move through, and the kind of precision that actually makes a difference.

  • 20+ years styling
  • BFA in Fashion Design
  • Tech and startup background, NYC
I don't tell people what to wear. They tell me, through our process using my signature method.

Process

The Process

Six steps, three phases. A method built to move from where you are to where you want to be.

Discover
01

Intake

A conversation about who you are, where you are headed, and what you want your presence to say. We cover your life, your work, and the gap between how you are seen and how you see yourself.

02

Audit

We go through what you own together. What you reach for, what you avoid, and what has been sitting untouched for years. No judgment. Just clarity about what is actually there.

Edit
03

Edit

We decide what stays and what goes. Every piece has a reason. The ones we keep become the foundation for everything that follows.

Express
04

Direction

You receive a clear style direction built around your actual life. Not a template. Not a mood board. A real framework you can use on your own, long after we are done.

05

Source

I research and find the pieces that fill the gaps. Each one is chosen to fit what you already have and what you are building toward. You are not doing any of the shopping.

06

Wear

You try things on. I am there to edit in real time. We refine until it feels like you, not like a version of someone else's taste.

Services

How
We
Work

Not sure where to start? That is part of the process. We focus on clarity, direction, and building a wardrobe that actually works for your life.

01

The Style Session

A focused session working exclusively with what you already own. We build outfits, identify patterns, and create clear direction for how to get dressed every day.

02

The Closet Edit

A full wardrobe review to decide what stays, what goes, and what is missing. We create structure, eliminate friction, and establish a strong foundation.

03

The Closet Edit and Build

Everything in The Closet Edit, plus a sourcing phase. We fill the gaps with considered pieces that align with your lifestyle, goals, and the wardrobe we built together.

04

Event Styling

You have a specific occasion and you want to feel right for it. We start with context, work through options, and land on something unmistakably you.

White Glove Retainer

Ongoing access to styling support as your life evolves. For clients who want a consistent partner, not a one-time service.
