Questions

The short answers.

What does the consultation cover?

We talk about your concept, your style, what you already own, how far you want to take it, and how much you want to spend on sourcing. No idea is too formed or too vague.

Do you source the whole costume?

Yes. After the consultation I handle all the research and sourcing. You get a list to confirm and shop from. You do not have to set foot in a store or spend hours searching online.

What if I have no idea what I want to be?

That is the ideal starting point. We build the brief from scratch in the consultation. Most of the best looks start with no idea at all.
